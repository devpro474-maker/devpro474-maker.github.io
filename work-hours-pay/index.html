Work Hours & Pay

A private, offline-first shift tracker for hourly workers. Record hours, estimate gross earnings and keep your own reports.

Read privacy policyGet support

Features

Track your shifts

Start and stop a timer, add previous shifts manually and edit saved entries.

Estimate your pay

Set an hourly rate, breaks and an overtime multiplier. Earnings are gross estimates before deductions, not a legal payroll calculation.

Control your records

Export Excel or CSV reports and back up saved work to JSON. Backups are not encrypted; keep them private.

Availability: Work Hours & Pay is currently in development and testing. A Google Play link will be added only after release approval.
