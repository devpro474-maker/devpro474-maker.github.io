Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · App: Work Hours & Pay · Site account: devpro474-maker

Development status: This is privacy information for the application while it is in testing. The final publisher identity, support email, third-party SDK disclosures and release details must be reviewed before Google Play publication. This page does not imply Google Play or AdMob approval.

1. What the app does

Work Hours & Pay records shift start and end times, breaks, hourly rates, currency labels and overtime settings. It estimates gross earnings before tax and other deductions. It is not a certified payroll or employment-law service.

2. Work records kept on your device

Shift records and pay settings are kept in private application storage on your Android device. The timer and calculator can work without internet. No account is required, and these records are not automatically sent to a developer-operated server.

3. Exported documents and backups

When exporting Excel (.xlsx) or CSV reports, or backing up work records as JSON, Android lets you choose where the file is saved. You can choose on-device storage or an independent cloud provider, which may sync your document under its own terms.

JSON backups include completed shifts and pay settings. They are not encrypted and do not include an unfinished timer. Restoring a backup replaces saved shifts and pay settings after an explicit confirmation. Exported/backup files remain at the chosen destination until separately deleted.

4. Advertising and third-party processing

The application integrates Google Mobile Ads (Next-Gen) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Subject to region, SDK settings and consent, these services may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app and advertising interactions, and app/SDK diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

Advertising data handling is separate from local work records. Ads need internet; time tracking does not. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's processing.

5. Advertising choices

Where required by UMP, the app presents a consent message and provides privacy options in Settings. Android's device-level privacy controls may also apply. Actual choices depend on location and release configuration.

6. Retention, access and deletion

You can edit or delete saved shifts in the app. Removing the app or clearing its data removes locally saved app records, but separate backup and report documents remain wherever you chose to save them. Google and external file providers follow their own retention practices.

7. Security and children

Records use Android private app storage. Exports and JSON backups are readable outside the app, and should be protected. This utility is not designed as a children's app; the final target-audience declaration will be determined before release.

8. Changes and contact

This page may be updated as the app or SDKs change. For privacy questions or deletion guidance, use the public GitHub support issue form. A GitHub account is required to post there and all issues are publicly visible. Do not disclose private work information or personal data in issues. A direct support contact will be added when verified by the publisher.
