Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Shift recording and gross-pay estimates work without internet. Ads need a connection.

Can I restore shifts?

Yes. JSON backup and restore is available for completed shifts and pay settings. Backups are not encrypted and restore requires confirmation.

Why are amounts estimates?

Pay calculations use user settings and exclude taxes, deductions and jurisdiction-specific labor rules.
