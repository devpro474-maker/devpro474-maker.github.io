TermTidy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to TermTidy: Study Planner for Android (com.devpro474.termtidy). Developer website account: devpro474-maker. For privacy questions or requests, contact devpro909390@gmail.com. This policy describes the app and its advertising-enabled editions.

Study records and local processing

TermTidy does not require an account. Semesters, subjects, classes, assignments, exams, study topics, study blocks, saved focus sessions, preferences and reminder scheduling information are stored on your device. The planner calculates schedules locally. Our application code does not send your academic records to a developer-operated server.

Internet access is required to use the planner. TermTidy checks the network status reported by Android. This connectivity check does not transmit academic records to a developer endpoint.

Advertising and third-party processing

Advertising-enabled editions use Google's Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Development editions may display test ads. Google may automatically collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device and account identifiers, app or ad interactions and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that data collected by its advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Advertising processing is subject to Google's policies and applicable privacy choices. Read Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and information about partner sites and apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . Advertising SDK data is not the same as the study records stored by the planner.

Where applicable, the app presents Google privacy messages and provides Advertising privacy options in Settings. You can also use Android's advertising ID controls. With a valid internet connection, availability of the planner does not depend on an ad being available or on consenting to personalized ads.

Permissions and notifications

Internet and network status permissions support connectivity and advertising. Notification permission allows local task and exam reminders; optional precise alarm access improves scheduling, and boot-related access allows reminders to be scheduled again following a restart. You can change notification and precise alarm permissions in Android settings. Reminders may show the titles of tasks or exams; Android's lock-screen privacy settings determine what is visible. Timing can be affected by the operating system.

Backups, retention and deletion

Study records remain on the device until you delete them, replace them using a backup, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to device behavior. Settings includes Delete all local records. Export backup creates a readable JSON file at a location you choose using Android's document picker. Restore backup replaces local records after confirmation. Keep exported files private and delete them separately when no longer needed. Storage providers you choose may apply their own privacy policies. Active timers and Android permissions are not transferred in the backup.

There is no TermTidy account or developer-hosted study database to delete. Advertising-related information handled by Google is retained according to Google's policies. Contact us if you have questions about exercising your privacy rights.

Support and security

If you contact us by email, your address, message and any attachments you provide are received for responding to your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary private study records. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to respond to and resolve inquiries or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of your support correspondence using the contact below, subject to applicable obligations.

Local records rely on Android's application storage protections. Exported JSON backups are readable and are not encrypted by TermTidy. No method of storing or transmitting information can guarantee complete security.

Audience

TermTidy is designed for higher-education study planning and is not directed specifically at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or other interactions with the app, contact us so we can address the concern.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its privacy practices change. The updated policy will display its revision date. For questions, access or deletion requests regarding information you have provided to us, contact devpro909390@gmail.com.
